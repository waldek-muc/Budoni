# Welcome to Our Apartment in Budoni!

Welcome to our apartment in Budoni! We are thrilled to host you in beautiful Sardinia. To help you settle in quickly and make the most of your time here, we’ve put together this quick guide. It contains everything you need to know about how the apartment works—from the strict local recycling rules to the heating—as well as a list of our favorite nearby beaches and restaurants. Please make yourself at home, and we hope you have a relaxing and unforgettable holiday!

### How to Enter the Apartment

When you arrive, the exterior shutters will be lowered, blocking the door. To raise them, locate the switch on the pillar to the left (when facing the shutters). Use the switch to open the shutters, which will then give you access to the front glass door.

### Parking Space

We have a dedicated parking space. You can see it directly from the terrace—it is the one on the left.

### Heating & Hot Water

The apartment uses gas heating. The gas bottles (*bombole*) are located in a small compartment on the right-hand side of the main entrance. To open this compartment, you will need the small red pliers located on a shelf in the storage room, near the safe box.

You generally shouldn't need to access this, but if a bottle runs out of gas, there are two of them available. If the active one runs out, you will need to close its valve and open the backup one.

Please keep in mind that it takes some time for hot water to reach the taps or shower, so give it a moment before assuming the gas has run out. The best way to check for an issue is to look at the control display in the storage room. During normal operation, it shows the water temperature. If the connected bottle runs out of gas, the display will indicate this. Please check this display if you suspect the gas is empty.

### Waste Collection & Recycling

In Sardinia, waste segregation is strictly enforced. You must sort your waste correctly and follow a specific collection schedule.

Budoni's services categorize waste into 5 types:

* **Organic (Umido):** Must be placed in biodegradable bags (the shopping bags you receive at local supermarkets like EuroSpar or MD are suitable).
* **General Waste (Secco):** Place in the dedicated container.
* **Paper (Carta):** Place in the dedicated container.
* **Glass and Cans (Vetro e Lattine):** Place in the dedicated container.
* **Plastic (Plastica):** Does not have a dedicated container. It must be placed in **transparent** plastic bags (this is important!).

Waste is collected every day except Sunday. However, only specific types of waste are collected each day, so you will need to follow the schedule.

**Instructions:** Please place the appropriate container or transparent plastic bag outside, in front of the terrace gate on the street. Do this the evening before, as collections happen early in the morning.

**Pro Tip:** We highly recommend installing the **Junker app** ([https://junkerapp.it/en/](https://junkerapp.it/en/)) on your phone. It shows the daily schedule, sends reminders to put the bins out, and even allows you to scan a product's barcode to see which bin it belongs in.

### Beaches

If you visit in August, especially in the second half of the month, parking can be difficult to find if you like to sleep in. During August, I recommend arriving at the beach between 09:00 and 09:30 to secure a good spot. Most of the beaches described below are very long, so if you don't mind walking a bit, you will always find a place.

* **Spiaggia di Budoni:** The local beach. Typical for Sardinia, it features fine sand and crystal-clear water. There are three entrances; the two farthest from the apartment have restaurants. Note that after a storm or strong winds, there might be some seaweed on the beach or in the water.
* **Spiaggia di Su Tirazu -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/6hzWcotXFmDyii7v9](https://maps.app.goo.gl/6hzWcotXFmDyii7v9): About 15 minutes away by car. It has slightly larger-grain sand and crystal-clear water. It is a great spot for water sports and home to the Nido Surf School.
* **Spiaggia di Cala Suaraccia -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/fWDwrb5hAqSB89fZ9](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fWDwrb5hAqSB89fZ9): One of our favorite beaches! Located 30 minutes away, it offers a great view of Tavolara and is well-protected from the wind. It is rather small and can get crowded (and is a bit more expensive), so you need to arrive early if visiting in August.
* **Cala Brandinchi -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/QtmMXbLNh9cojZit9](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QtmMXbLNh9cojZit9): One of the most popular beaches in the area. You must book a spot in advance ([https://santeodorospiagge.it/en/](https://santeodorospiagge.it/en/)), which is actually beneficial because it prevents overcrowding. The booking fee is only €2 per person. What makes this beach special is that the water is very shallow—you can walk 100m into the sea, making it excellent for small children. The beach itself is quite narrow, though.
* **Spiaggia di Berchida -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/1BRwGhRFuT5yz6af6](https://maps.app.goo.gl/1BRwGhRFuT5yz6af6): This one is farther away, about a 40-minute drive. It used to be a wild beach, but it now has good infrastructure and can attract quite a few people. The beach itself is beautiful and very long (it's actually 2 or 3 beaches connected together). The water is crystal clear.

### Grocery Shops

* **EuroSpar Budoni -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/DmicQ4DwTtToSxQN6](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DmicQ4DwTtToSxQN6): A very well-stocked grocery store where you can find almost everything you need, including fresh fish and beef for barbecuing.
* **MD -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/RhBd7FkuLidJZTWH8](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RhBd7FkuLidJZTWH8): A discount supermarket with a decent selection and cheap prices.
* **Sidis:** A medium-sized grocery store. This shop changed its name this year, so it might not be visible on Google Maps just yet.
* **Acqua & Sapone -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/prdenr6gKAFrVVx6A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/prdenr6gKAFrVVx6A): A drugstore/chemist. If you run out of shampoo, shower gel, or toothpaste, this is the place to go. It is quite large and well-stocked.

### Restaurants

* **Yamaki Sushi -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/QWWovkv7aUEq7d6J9](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QWWovkv7aUEq7d6J9): Our son's favorite! :-) They serve very good, fresh, all-you-can-eat sushi. * **Lu Soli Gelateria -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/QAhiuyHQ4y96zMe7A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QAhiuyHQ4y96zMe7A): Very good ice cream with a large selection and good prices.
* **In Forno -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/RRN9axsRuGUXx9Yf9](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RRN9axsRuGUXx9Yf9): A takeaway pizzeria, though you can also eat there. They offer good pizzas at reasonable prices.
* **Pizzeria Marave -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/882nh8rTonUjWJmNA](https://maps.app.goo.gl/882nh8rTonUjWJmNA): Authentic Neapolitan pizza. Good quality, though in a slightly higher price range.
* **Mama's Restaurant -** [https://maps.app.goo.gl/ftY5hsfZgMNftEQcA](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ftY5hsfZgMNftEQcA): Typical Italian food with good quality, also in a slightly higher price range.

### Night Market

The Budoni Night Market runs from July to September. Local authorities block off a large part of Via Nazionale, and local artisans set up small stands selling all kinds of souvenirs, art, sweets, and local products. It's a great evening stroll!
